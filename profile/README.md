# FogCast 🌫️

**Weather and fog forecasts for Lake Constance**, a project of the Master of Computer Science program at HTWG Konstanz.

FogCast compares the forecasts of around 30 weather models, measures the real weather with its own station at the HTWG landing stage, and shows everything on a website and in an app.

🌐 **Website:** [fogcast.in.htwg-konstanz.de](https://fogcast.in.htwg-konstanz.de)
📖 **Start here:** [**FogCast repository**](https://github.com/FogCast-Konstanz/FogCast). Architecture, all repositories, setup, deployment and operations in one place.
